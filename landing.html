Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about PlanTrack.

How does the 7-Day Free Trial work?
Every new account automatically receives 7 full days of PRO access for free! No credit card is required to get started. After 7 days, you can choose to subscribe for just $0.50/month.
Do alarms ring when my app or browser tab is closed?
Yes! PlanTrack uses background Service Workers and Web Notification TimestampTriggers so system alarms and plan reminders ring loud with vibration sound even when your browser or tab is completely closed.
Can I share plans and timetables with friends or team members?
Absolutely. Inside Nexus Chat, you can attach any plan or timetable. Receivers see an interactive preview card with a 1-click "Import to My Account" button to save it instantly.
How do voice and video calls work?
Inside Nexus Chat, click the Phone or Video icon. If the friend is online, it connects a real-time call with live camera video and microphone audio. If the friend is offline, it leaves a missed call message.
